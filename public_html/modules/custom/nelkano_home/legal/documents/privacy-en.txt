Nelkano and SDK Studio privacy
Updated: 31 October 2026

Controller and contact

The data controller is Iván Peláez García, responsible for the Nelkano project.
For questions and requests about your data: contacto@nelkano.com.
Nelkano is a free project and currently receives no income.

You can play without creating an account. Your library and saves are kept on
your device. Connected features send the data needed to provide the service
when you choose to use them, as explained below.

Library and local use

Nelkano stores imported games, their names and identifiers, paths, covers,
settings, saves and save states on your device. Creating an account does not
upload all these files. You can remove them using the application's controls.
Copies you export to another folder or device must also be removed from
their respective locations.

Account, profile and friends

If you register, we process your email, access information, account identifier,
profile and friendship requests to create and protect the account and operate
the features you request. Your chosen public name, picture, color and status
may be shown to other users. Friend search can locate accounts by name or
email. Review the profile information you share with other people.
Authorized administrators can access the information needed to maintain
the service.

Account age

You must be 14 or older to create an account. If the law in your country requires
a higher age to consent to the use of your data, that age applies. Local features
remain available without an account. We do not request a date of birth, identity
document or age confirmation when signing in. If an account does not meet the
applicable age, we will restrict its use and handle its deletion. For questions
or account requests, contact contacto@nelkano.com.

Synchronized activity

When you use synchronized activity or profile features, the server may receive
library and device identifiers, system, favorites, play times and usage counters.
This information is distinct from complete game or save files. Review the
activity and profile options before enabling synchronization or publication.

Connections, multiplayer and streaming

Connected features process account or room identifiers, connection signals,
messages and the technical data needed to connect participants. Starting a
broadcast shares the game's picture and sound with the service's recipients.
Participants and connection providers may receive technical network information,
including your IP address. Do not start a broadcast containing information
you do not wish to share.

Google STUN (stun.l.google.com) is used to discover connections. The WebRTC path
connects participants; some multiplayer features also share game states. The
Android HTTP fallback stores the latest video frame and audio block on the
server, replacing them with subsequent ones. These files use private storage;
the old public route is blocked. Closing or expiring a session prevents further
use, but does not mean its files and signals are physically erased immediately.
Periodic cleanup removes closed or expired connections, their video-frame and
audio files, and signalling messages older than two hours.

Error reports

The optional Reports module lets you send Nelkano a report with your account,
problem description and technical information. Depending on your authorization,
it may attach a screenshot and a game save or save state. The report includes
the technical fields shown in the form; it does not collect arbitrary device
logs. The form does not request the game file as a separate attachment. Save
states may include memory and game content. Review the form
before confirming: these files can contain names or other information from
your game. Opening the form does not by itself authorize sending it.
Reports and attachments are used for diagnosis by authorized project staff;
they are not automatically published as downloads.

Modules and SDK Studio

Each module can request permissions to use local data or connect to servers.
Review its author, permissions and information before installing it. Authors'
external services have their own terms. Disabling a module prevents new
operations by that module but does not by itself delete information it has
already sent to an external server. SDK Studio stores projects, settings and
signing keys locally. Its connection testing features can make real connections
when used. Do not publish private keys or personal information in your modules.

Optional Google Drive

If you install the optional Drive module and authorize your Google account,
you can transfer the games and saves you select to your own private Drive.
This file transfer is distinct from synchronizing activity metadata with
Nelkano. Your Google account and its authorization are independent of your
Nelkano account. You can manage copies and revoke access through Google;
revoking access does not by itself delete files already stored in Drive.

Website and visit measurement

The website processes request information such as IP address, date, browser
and security logs to serve pages and downloads and prevent abuse. Email and
contact form messages are used to respond to you. Website Google Analytics
loads only if you accept measurement. It may process browser identifiers,
visited pages and download clicks. A click does not prove installation.
You can withdraw consent through Configure cookies. Website measurement is
distinct from tracking game sessions within the application.

The Analytics property is configured to retain event data for 2 months and
user data for 14 months. New activity resets the user identifier's retention
period, so continued visits can extend it. These limits do not cover all
standard aggregated reports. Expired data is deleted through Google's
periodic process.
https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196?hl=en

The website uses the analytics cookies _ga to distinguish browsers and
_ga_<identifier> to retain session state. Their configured expiry is 63,072,000
seconds (two nominal years) from creation, without automatically extending it
on every visit. Browsers can impose shorter limits. Cookie expiry is distinct
from retention of events in Google Analytics. The analytics choice is stored
locally for up to 730 days with its date and notice version; a changed notice
or expiry requires a new choice before enabling analytics. You can change it
earlier through Configure cookies.

Purposes and grounds for processing

Data needed for your account and requested features is processed to provide
that service. Communications are used to handle your request. Security and
abuse prevention rely on the legitimate interest in protecting the service,
with respect for your rights. Optional analytics and optional attachments rely
on your consent. Applicable legal obligations will also be observed. Without
the information needed by a connected feature, that feature may be unavailable;
playing without an account remains possible.

Recipients and external services

The controller and authorized maintainers process account, activity and report
data needed to provide those features. Hosting, email, infrastructure and
connection providers may process the data needed to operate the service.
Participants in social or connection features receive the data shared with them.

Hostinger hosts the website, database and files under Premium Web Hosting;
the contact mailbox uses Hostinger Free Business Email. Contact form messages
are sent to the mailbox to answer you, rather than a dedicated form database
table. The web server is in the Netherlands and its indicated backup location
is Lithuania. Email is a separate service; retention depends on that service
and the handling of enquiries. The provider’s data processing agreement is at:
https://www.hostinger.com/legal/dpa
Google receives website analytics if you accept it. The Drive module sends Google
the files you select and authorize. Other modules may communicate with providers
identified by their author.
Some services may process data outside the European Economic Area. You can ask
the privacy contact to identify the providers, the transfers affecting a feature
and information about, or a copy of, applicable safeguards. This notice does not
claim that all providers host data exclusively in the EU. Google information:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks
Your personal data is not sold. Data will be disclosed to authorities when
required by law.

Retention and account deletion

Account, profile, friend and activity data is retained as needed for those
features. You can delete your account using the deletion control and current
password, or ask contacto@nelkano.com for help. Administrator accounts require manual
handling to protect the service. Deletion removes the associated server-side
account data, sessions, social relationships, activity, avatar, reports and
your attachments. It does not remove your local library, exports, Drive files
or copies held by other people.

API access tokens last one hour and refresh tokens thirty days. Revocation or
expiry invalidates access; its database row is not necessarily removed at that
instant. Verification and recovery links last 24 hours. Pending multiplayer
connections expire after 5 minutes, and active game and streaming connections
after 2 hours, renewed by activity where applicable. Periodic cleanup removes
closed or expired connections and refresh-token rows that expired more than
24 hours ago. Physical deletion may require several passes.

Messages and reports are retained as needed to answer or investigate; that need
is reviewed when the enquiry or issue closes. Maintenance working copies must
be deleted when no longer needed. A fixed retention period in days has not
been verified for hosting or email logs. Drupal technical logs and inactive
website sessions older than 30 days are removed by periodic cleanup. This period
does not cover the hosting or email provider’s own logs.

Hostinger keeps weekly backups subject to the rotation of the contracted plan.
Instant selective deletion from every backup is not promised. A minimal private
record of the deleted account identifier and request date, without email or
password, is kept separately from restorable site backups. Before reopening a
restored site, the register is checked and the necessary deletions are reapplied.
It must be retained while backups or
restorations could recover that account. Local files remain until you remove
them; signing out does not delete them. Exceptional retention required by law
is limited to what is necessary and subject to restricted access.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and, where
applicable, portability, and withdraw consent, at contacto@nelkano.com. Describe
your request and the affected account or information. Proportionate identity
verification may be needed to protect your information. You may complain to
the Spanish Data Protection Agency: https://www.aepd.es/
